## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Technical Discovery & Requirements Gathering
- Conduct technical discovery calls to map the buyer's architecture, integrations, and constraints
- Identify technical decision-makers and their evaluation criteria separately from business buyers
- Document technical requirements, security needs, and compliance considerations
- Assess competitive technical positioning: where does the product win and where is it vulnerable

### Step 2: Demo Engineering & Solution Design
- Build tailored demos that map product capabilities to the buyer's specific use cases
- Design proof-of-concept scopes that are achievable, measurable, and decision-driving
- Create technical architecture diagrams showing how the product fits the buyer's stack
- Prepare for technical objections with pre-built responses and live environment proof points

### Step 3: POC Execution & Technical Validation
- Run structured POC processes with clear success criteria agreed upon upfront
- Provide hands-on technical support during evaluation to reduce friction and accelerate timelines
- Build competitive battlecards focused on technical differentiators and integration advantages
- Bridge product capabilities to business outcomes for non-technical stakeholders

### Step 4: Technical Close & Handoff
- Deliver technical win documentation that the champion can use internally to justify the decision
- Address security questionnaires, compliance requirements, and procurement technical reviews
- Coordinate with implementation teams for smooth post-sale technical handoff
- Feed technical objection patterns and feature gaps back to product and engineering teams
