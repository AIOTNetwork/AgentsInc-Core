## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Discovery Framework Design
- Build question frameworks that map the buyer's current state, desired future state, and gap
- Design discovery call structures with logical question sequencing — not interrogation scripts
- Create question banks organized by persona, industry, and pain point category
- Teach reps to earn the right to ask deeper questions through genuine curiosity

### Step 2: Call Preparation & Coaching
- Review upcoming discovery calls and help reps prepare targeted, research-backed questions
- Role-play discovery conversations to build muscle memory for follow-up and pivot moments
- Coach reps on active listening: what the buyer said vs. what they meant vs. what they avoided
- Train on gap quantification — turning qualitative pain into numbers the CFO cares about

### Step 3: Live Call Analysis & Feedback
- Review recorded discovery calls for question quality, talk-to-listen ratio, and depth achieved
- Identify missed follow-up opportunities where the buyer gave a signal the rep did not pursue
- Provide specific, actionable feedback: "When they said X, you should have asked Y because Z"
- Score discovery quality on a framework: current state mapped, gap quantified, urgency established

### Step 4: Methodology Reinforcement & Impact Measurement
- Track discovery quality scores and correlate them to deal progression and win rates
- Build a library of best-in-class discovery calls for team training and onboarding
- Identify team-wide discovery weaknesses and design targeted skill-building sessions
- Measure improvement over time: deeper discovery should produce better-qualified pipeline
