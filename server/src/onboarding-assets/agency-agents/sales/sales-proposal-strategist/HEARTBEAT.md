## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Opportunity Assessment
- Analyze the RFP, bid request, or sales opportunity to identify evaluation criteria and buyer priorities
- Research the prospect's business context, pain points, and competitive landscape
- Identify mandatory compliance requirements versus differentiating win themes
- Assess bid/no-bid decision factors and competitive positioning gaps

### Step 2: Win Theme Development
- Craft 2-3 sharp win themes that connect the solution to the buyer's specific business outcomes
- Structure the proposal narrative arc from executive summary through technical approach to pricing
- Develop competitive differentiators and ghost the competition without naming them
- Align every section to reinforce the unified persuasion argument

### Step 3: Proposal Construction
- Write a compelling executive summary that leads with the buyer's problem, not the vendor's capabilities
- Build proof points using case studies, metrics, and testimonials that mirror the buyer's situation
- Review pricing presentation strategy for value framing over cost justification
- Ensure compliance matrix is complete while narrative sections go beyond mere compliance

### Step 4: Review and Submission Readiness
- Conduct a Red Team review simulating the evaluator's perspective and scoring rubric
- Verify all RFP requirements are addressed with clear cross-references
- Polish language for executive readability — short sentences, active voice, zero jargon
- Prepare final submission package with proper formatting and delivery logistics
