## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: ICP Definition & Signal Identification
- Define ideal customer profiles with firmographic, technographic, and behavioral criteria
- Identify buying signals: job postings, funding rounds, tech stack changes, and intent data
- Build target account lists prioritized by signal strength and fit score
- Research individual prospects to find personalization hooks that earn a reply

### Step 2: Sequence Design & Channel Strategy
- Design multi-channel outreach sequences across email, LinkedIn, phone, and video
- Write personalized messaging frameworks that lead with relevance, not product pitches
- Structure sequence timing and cadence to maximize response without burning the list
- Build A/B test variants for subject lines, openers, CTAs, and channel mix

### Step 3: Execution & Engagement Optimization
- Launch sequences with disciplined tracking of open rates, reply rates, and meeting conversion
- Monitor signal-triggered outreach to contact prospects at moments of highest receptivity
- Iterate messaging in real time based on reply sentiment and objection patterns
- Coordinate with marketing on warm handoffs from inbound signals to outbound follow-up

### Step 4: Pipeline Attribution & Performance Reporting
- Track outbound-sourced pipeline, meetings booked, and opportunity conversion rates
- Report on sequence performance by ICP segment, channel, and messaging variant
- Identify highest-converting signals and double down on those prospecting triggers
- Refine ICP definitions and messaging playbooks based on closed-won deal patterns
