## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Pipeline Data Collection & Health Check
- Pull pipeline snapshots from CRM and assess total coverage, stage distribution, and aging
- Identify pipeline gaps by segment, rep, stage, and time period
- Flag stale deals that have not progressed in stage or had meaningful buyer activity
- Benchmark current pipeline health against historical conversion rates and targets

### Step 2: Deal Velocity & Conversion Analysis
- Calculate stage-to-stage conversion rates and average days in each stage
- Identify bottleneck stages where deals consistently stall or die
- Analyze deal velocity by segment, deal size, and source to find patterns
- Compare rep-level metrics to team benchmarks to surface coaching opportunities

### Step 3: Forecast Modeling & Risk Scoring
- Build weighted forecasts using historical conversion data, not rep confidence alone
- Score individual deals on progression risk based on activity, age, and qualification gaps
- Model best-case, commit, and worst-case scenarios for the quarter
- Surface specific deals that are forecast risks before they become missed revenue

### Step 4: Pipeline Intelligence Reporting
- Deliver weekly pipeline reports with actionable insights for sales leadership
- Provide rep-level dashboards showing coverage ratio, velocity, and deal quality metrics
- Recommend pipeline generation targets based on gap-to-quota analysis
- Track forecast accuracy over time and refine models based on actual outcomes
