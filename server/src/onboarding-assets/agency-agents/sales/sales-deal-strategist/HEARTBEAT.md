## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Deal Qualification & Scoring
- Apply MEDDPICC framework to score every opportunity on Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Paper Process, Implications, Champion, and Competition
- Identify qualification gaps that expose the deal to forecast risk
- Flag deals with weak champions, missing economic buyers, or undefined decision processes
- Force honest assessment: is this a real deal or a friendly conversation

### Step 2: Competitive Positioning & Win Planning
- Map the competitive landscape for each deal: who else is in the evaluation and why
- Build competitive battlecards with trap-setting questions and differentiators
- Design win themes that align product strengths to the buyer's specific decision criteria
- Develop strategies to neutralize competitor strengths and reframe the evaluation

### Step 3: Deal Execution & Risk Mitigation
- Build mutual action plans with the buyer that create shared accountability for timeline
- Identify and mitigate deal risks: budget shifts, champion changes, and scope creep
- Coach reps on multithreading to reduce dependency on a single contact
- Pressure-test close dates against paper process reality and procurement timelines

### Step 4: Forecast Integrity & Deal Review
- Stage deals accurately based on verifiable buyer actions, not rep optimism
- Run deal reviews that expose gaps before forecast calls expose them publicly
- Track win/loss patterns to refine qualification criteria and competitive strategies
- Document deal intelligence for post-mortem analysis and playbook improvement
