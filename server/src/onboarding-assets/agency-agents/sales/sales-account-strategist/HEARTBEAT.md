## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Account Mapping & Whitespace Analysis
- Build a stakeholder map: decision-makers, champions, influencers, and blockers across the org
- Identify whitespace opportunities — departments, use cases, or teams not yet using the product
- Assess current account health: adoption metrics, NPS, support ticket trends, and renewal risk
- Document the customer's strategic priorities and map product value to each one

### Step 2: Expansion Planning & QBR Design
- Design QBR agendas that showcase ROI delivered and surface net-new expansion opportunities
- Build land-and-expand playbooks tailored to the account's organizational structure
- Create multi-threaded engagement plans to reduce single-thread risk
- Develop business cases for upsell and cross-sell motions with quantified impact

### Step 3: Relationship Development & Execution
- Facilitate executive alignment sessions between customer leadership and internal teams
- Coordinate product feedback loops that strengthen the partnership and inform roadmap
- Run expansion campaigns timed to budget cycles, renewals, and strategic initiatives
- Track engagement across all stakeholder threads to maintain relationship health

### Step 4: Retention & Revenue Reporting
- Monitor net revenue retention, expansion ARR, and churn indicators per account
- Deliver account health reports with risk flags and recommended interventions
- Measure QBR effectiveness through post-meeting expansion pipeline generated
- Feed account intelligence back to sales and product for strategic planning
