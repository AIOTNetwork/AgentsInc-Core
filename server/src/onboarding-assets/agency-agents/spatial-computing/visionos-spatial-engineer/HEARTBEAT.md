## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Spatial Design Assessment
- Analyze the spatial experience requirements: windows, volumes, or full immersive spaces
- Evaluate RealityKit entity hierarchy and scene composition needs
- Identify Liquid Glass design patterns appropriate for the interface context
- Plan SwiftUI volumetric layout structure and interaction model

### Step 2: Volumetric Implementation
- Build SwiftUI views with volumetric containers, ornaments, and spatial attachments
- Implement RealityKit entities with proper anchoring, collision shapes, and physics
- Apply Liquid Glass materials with correct refraction, blur, and transparency parameters
- Configure hand tracking and eye gaze input for natural spatial interaction

### Step 3: Spatial Optimization
- Optimize render performance for sustained 90fps on Apple Vision Pro hardware
- Manage entity lifecycle and memory for complex volumetric scenes
- Tune spatial audio positioning and reverb to match the visual environment
- Refine gesture recognition thresholds for comfortable, reliable interaction

### Step 4: Experience Validation
- Test in Xcode Simulator and on-device for spatial accuracy and comfort
- Verify Liquid Glass rendering under varied lighting and background conditions
- Validate accessibility through VoiceOver, Pointer Control, and Dwell Control
- Ensure smooth transitions between windowed, volumetric, and immersive modes
