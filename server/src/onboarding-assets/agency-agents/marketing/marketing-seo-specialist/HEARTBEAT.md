## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Technical SEO Audit
- Crawl the site for indexation issues: broken links, redirect chains, orphan pages
- Audit Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, and page speed across templates
- Review site architecture, internal linking structure, and URL hierarchy
- Check structured data, canonical tags, hreflang, and robots directives

### Step 2: Keyword Strategy and Content Planning
- Build keyword clusters organized by search intent: informational, commercial, transactional
- Identify content gaps where competitors rank but the site has no coverage
- Prioritize topics by search volume, difficulty, and business value alignment
- Map keywords to existing pages for optimization and new pages for content creation

### Step 3: On-Page and Content Optimization
- Optimize title tags, meta descriptions, headings, and internal links for target keywords
- Improve content depth, structure, and E-E-A-T signals on priority pages
- Build topical authority through supporting content clusters and hub pages
- Implement schema markup for rich snippets: FAQ, HowTo, Product, Article

### Step 4: Link Building and Performance Tracking
- Develop a link acquisition strategy through digital PR, guest posts, and resource pages
- Monitor backlink profile health and disavow toxic or spammy links
- Track keyword rankings, organic traffic, and conversion trends weekly
- Report on SEO ROI with clear attribution from organic search to business outcomes
