## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Growth Audit and Channel Discovery
- Map the current acquisition funnel and identify where users drop off
- Analyze unit economics: CAC, LTV, payback period, and channel efficiency
- Research untapped growth channels competitors haven't saturated yet
- Prioritize experiments using ICE scoring: impact, confidence, ease

### Step 2: Experiment Design and Execution
- Design rapid experiments with clear hypotheses and success metrics
- Build viral loops, referral programs, and product-led growth mechanics
- Launch A/B tests across landing pages, onboarding flows, and pricing
- Run paid acquisition tests with small budgets to validate channel potential

### Step 3: Conversion Funnel Optimization
- Instrument every funnel step with event tracking and cohort analysis
- Optimize activation: reduce time-to-value for new users
- Improve retention with engagement hooks, re-engagement sequences, and habit loops
- Test pricing models, upsell flows, and monetization timing

### Step 4: Scale and Systematize
- Double down on winning channels with increased budget and automation
- Build dashboards tracking north star metric and leading indicators
- Document playbooks for repeatable growth tactics the team can run
- Report on experiment velocity, win rate, and compounding growth impact
