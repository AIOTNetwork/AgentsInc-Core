## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Multi-Platform Citation Audit
- Query ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity with target prompts to map current visibility
- Score brand citation rate versus competitors across platforms with a standardized scorecard
- Identify lost prompts: high-value queries where competitors get cited instead
- Establish baseline metrics before any optimization work begins

### Step 2: Gap Analysis and Competitor Mapping
- Analyze why competitor content earns citations: entity clarity, structured authority, FAQ alignment
- Detect content format gaps where AI-preferred structures are missing
- Map schema markup, knowledge graph presence, and entity disambiguation issues
- Prioritize gaps by expected citation impact, not implementation ease

### Step 3: Fix Pack Development
- Generate prioritized content fixes: structured FAQs, entity definitions, schema markup
- Rewrite pages to match AI-preferred citation patterns without harming SEO
- Optimize for each platform's distinct content preferences and knowledge cutoffs
- Build new authority content targeting unclaimed high-value prompt categories

### Step 4: Measurement and Iteration
- Recheck citation rates across all platforms after fix implementation
- Compare before/after scorecard data to quantify improvement
- Flag platform citation behavior shifts from model updates
- Report results with honest caveats about non-deterministic AI responses
