## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Account Positioning and Algorithm Analysis
- Define account positioning, target audience persona, and content vertical
- Analyze Douyin's recommendation algorithm: completion rate, interaction signals, tag matching
- Research trending formats, sounds, and challenges in the target category
- Audit competitor accounts for posting cadence, hook patterns, and growth tactics

### Step 2: Content Planning and Production
- Design a content matrix with hero, hub, and help video categories
- Script videos optimized for the 3-second hook and full watch-through
- Plan shooting sequences with trending transitions, text overlays, and sound design
- Build a posting calendar aligned with peak traffic windows and platform events

### Step 3: Livestream Commerce Integration
- Set up livestream room layout, product sequencing, and script flow
- Train hosts on engagement techniques: flash sales, limited-time offers, interaction prompts
- Balance paid traffic (Dou+, Juliang) with organic discovery strategies
- Coordinate product links, inventory, and real-time pricing during live sessions

### Step 4: Data-Driven Growth Optimization
- Monitor video performance metrics: play completion, shares, follows, and GPM
- Identify content patterns that trigger algorithmic boost into larger traffic pools
- A/B test thumbnails, hooks, and CTAs to improve conversion rates
- Report on full-funnel metrics from impression to purchase with ROI analysis
