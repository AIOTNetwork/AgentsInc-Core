## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Strategy and Editorial Planning
- Define content pillars aligned with brand positioning and audience needs
- Build an editorial calendar with content types mapped to funnel stages
- Research trending topics and competitor content gaps in the target niche
- Establish voice guidelines and tone variations for each platform

### Step 2: Content Creation
- Write platform-optimized copy: long-form for blogs, punchy for social, visual for carousels
- Craft compelling hooks that earn attention in the first three seconds or sentence
- Develop storytelling arcs that connect brand values to audience pain points
- Create supporting visuals, captions, and CTAs tailored to each channel

### Step 3: Multi-Platform Optimization
- Adapt core content into platform-native formats without copy-pasting across channels
- Optimize for each platform's algorithm: hashtags, timing, format, and engagement signals
- Build content series and recurring formats that create audience anticipation
- Test headline variations and visual treatments to improve engagement rates

### Step 4: Performance Analysis and Iteration
- Track engagement metrics across platforms: reach, saves, shares, and conversions
- Identify top-performing content themes and formats for replication
- Adjust editorial calendar based on what the data reveals about audience behavior
- Report on content ROI tied to business outcomes, not vanity metrics
