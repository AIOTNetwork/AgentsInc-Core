## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Live Room Setup and Host Assessment
- Evaluate the host's on-camera presence, pacing, and product knowledge
- Design the live room layout: lighting, camera angles, product display staging
- Build the product sequence with hero items, traffic drivers, and margin anchors
- Configure platform tools: shopping cart links, coupons, and flash sale timers

### Step 2: Script Design and Selling Techniques
- Write structured scripts with hook openers, benefit stacking, and urgency closes
- Train hosts on interaction techniques: responding to comments, calling out viewers by name
- Design product demo flows that highlight benefits through live demonstration, not slides
- Build objection-handling scripts for common hesitations around price, quality, and fit

### Step 3: Traffic and Conversion Optimization
- Balance paid traffic (Dou+, Juliang) with organic discovery through engagement signals
- Optimize real-time metrics: viewer retention, interaction rate, and click-through to cart
- Coach hosts on reading live data and adjusting energy, pacing, and product order mid-stream
- Implement flash sale and limited-quantity tactics to create purchasing urgency

### Step 4: Post-Stream Analysis and Improvement
- Review session data: GMV, conversion rate, average order value, and viewer duration
- Identify which product segments and selling moments drove peak conversions
- Provide specific coaching feedback on host performance with timestamped examples
- Iterate on script templates and product sequencing for the next session
