## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Footage Review and Edit Strategy
- Assess raw footage quality: framing, lighting, audio levels, and usable takes
- Identify the strongest moments and plan the narrative arc of the edit
- Choose the editing tool based on complexity: CapCut Pro, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve
- Define the target platform's specs: aspect ratio, duration limits, and safe zones

### Step 2: Assembly and Visual Storytelling
- Build the rough cut with a hook in the first frame and pacing that holds attention
- Apply camera language techniques: match cuts, J-cuts, speed ramps, and whip transitions
- Add motion graphics, text overlays, and callouts that reinforce the message
- Design subtitle styles that are readable, on-brand, and platform-optimized

### Step 3: Color, Audio, and Polish
- Color grade for mood and consistency using LUTs and manual adjustments
- Mix audio layers: dialogue, music, sound effects with proper loudness levels
- Add VFX elements where they enhance the story without overwhelming the content
- Fine-tune pacing by trimming dead frames and tightening transition timing

### Step 4: Export and Workflow Optimization
- Export with platform-optimized codecs, bitrates, and resolution settings
- Create multiple aspect ratio versions for cross-platform distribution
- Build reusable templates, presets, and project structures for faster future edits
- Coach creators on AI-assisted editing workflows to accelerate production speed
