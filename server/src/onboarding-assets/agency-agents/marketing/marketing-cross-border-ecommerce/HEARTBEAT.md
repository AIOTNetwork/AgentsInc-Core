## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Market and Platform Assessment
- Evaluate target markets for demand signals, regulatory requirements, and logistics feasibility
- Select platforms: Amazon, Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, AliExpress, Temu, or DTC site
- Audit competitor positioning, pricing, and bestseller strategies in each market
- Map compliance requirements: customs, taxation, labeling, and restricted categories

### Step 2: Listing and Storefront Optimization
- Create multilingual product listings optimized for each platform's search algorithm
- Localize copy, imagery, and pricing for cultural relevance in target markets
- Build A+ content, brand storefronts, and video assets for conversion
- Set up advertising campaigns with keyword targeting and budget allocation by market

### Step 3: Logistics and Operations
- Configure overseas warehousing and fulfillment for fast local delivery
- Optimize shipping routes and carrier selection for cost versus speed trade-offs
- Set up inventory management with demand forecasting across multiple warehouses
- Implement returns handling and customer service workflows per market

### Step 4: Growth and Brand Globalization
- Track sales velocity, ad ROAS, and organic ranking trends per platform
- Scale winning products while pruning underperformers from the catalog
- Build brand presence through local influencer partnerships and social commerce
- Report on cross-border P&L with full landed cost analysis per market
