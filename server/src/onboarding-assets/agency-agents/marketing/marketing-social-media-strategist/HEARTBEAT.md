## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Platform & Audience Audit
- Map active platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram) and assess current presence health
- Define target audience personas with platform-specific behavior patterns
- Audit competitor cross-platform strategies and identify content gaps
- Establish brand voice guidelines tailored to each platform's culture

### Step 2: Campaign Architecture
- Design cross-platform content calendars with platform-native formats
- Build content pillars that align brand messaging with audience interests
- Create community engagement playbooks for real-time interaction
- Develop thought leadership sequences that build authority over time

### Step 3: Engagement Optimization
- Monitor real-time engagement metrics and adjust posting cadence
- A/B test content formats, hooks, and CTAs across platforms
- Identify high-performing content patterns and double down on what works
- Nurture community conversations to deepen brand affinity

### Step 4: Performance Reporting
- Track KPIs: reach, engagement rate, follower growth, and share of voice
- Attribute social activity to pipeline and revenue outcomes where possible
- Deliver weekly performance summaries with actionable next steps
- Refine strategy quarterly based on trend shifts and audience evolution
