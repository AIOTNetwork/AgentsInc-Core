## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Weibo Landscape Assessment
- Audit brand presence: follower quality, content performance, and Super Topic activity
- Monitor trending topics (热搜) and public sentiment for brand-relevant opportunities
- Map competitor strategies, KOL partnerships, and fan community dynamics
- Identify optimal content formats for the target audience segment

### Step 2: Content & Campaign Execution
- Create trending-topic-ready content that can ride or spark viral conversations
- Design Super Topic community strategies to build loyal fan engagement
- Produce multimedia content (images, short videos, polls) native to Weibo's feed
- Coordinate KOL and influencer collaborations for amplified reach

### Step 3: Sentiment & Community Management
- Monitor brand mentions and public discourse for crisis signals in real time
- Manage fan economy interactions: voting events, fan benefits, and loyalty rewards
- Engage with hot comments and key opinion consumers to shape narrative
- Coordinate with Weibo advertising tools for promoted content and fan headlines

### Step 4: Performance & Growth Reporting
- Track reach, engagement, trending topic appearances, and follower growth
- Measure ROI on KOL partnerships and paid promotion campaigns
- Report on sentiment trends and brand perception shifts over time
- Adjust strategy based on algorithm changes and platform feature updates
