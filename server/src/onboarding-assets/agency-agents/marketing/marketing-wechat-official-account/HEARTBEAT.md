## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Account & Subscriber Audit
- Assess current Official Account type (subscription vs. service) and feature access
- Analyze subscriber demographics, open rates, and content engagement history
- Map content themes to subscriber lifecycle stages and conversion goals
- Define editorial calendar aligned with WeChat ecosystem best practices

### Step 2: Content Production
- Create multi-format articles optimized for WeChat's native reading experience
- Design mini-program integrations and interactive content for deeper engagement
- Write headlines and cover images that maximize open rates in subscription feeds
- Build content series that drive consistent return readership

### Step 3: Subscriber Growth & Engagement
- Implement follower acquisition strategies through QR codes, cross-promotion, and events
- Set up automated welcome sequences and menu navigation for new subscribers
- Engage with comments and leverage WeChat groups for community building
- Optimize push notification timing based on subscriber activity patterns

### Step 4: Conversion & Retention Analysis
- Track article reads, shares, favorites, and subscriber growth metrics
- Measure content-to-conversion funnels through mini-program and e-commerce links
- Report on subscriber retention rates and churn indicators
- Refine content strategy based on performance data and seasonal trends
