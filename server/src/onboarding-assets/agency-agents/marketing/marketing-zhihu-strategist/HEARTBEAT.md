## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Topic & Authority Mapping
- Identify high-traffic questions in the brand's domain with unanswered or weak responses
- Audit existing Zhihu (知乎) presence: answer quality, upvotes, and follower credibility
- Map competitor knowledge-sharing strategies and content gaps to exploit
- Define expertise pillars that align brand authority with user search intent

### Step 2: Answer & Article Crafting
- Write long-form, evidence-backed answers that demonstrate genuine expertise
- Structure responses with clear logic: thesis, supporting data, practical takeaway
- Create Zhihu columns and articles for evergreen thought leadership content
- Optimize content for Zhihu's recommendation algorithm and Baidu search indexing

### Step 3: Community Credibility Building
- Engage with comments and follow-up questions to deepen answer visibility
- Participate in Zhihu Salt Club, Live sessions, and paid consultation features
- Build upvote momentum through quality signals rather than artificial engagement
- Cross-reference answers to create interconnected knowledge authority

### Step 4: Impact & Growth Analysis
- Track answer views, upvotes, collections, and follower growth metrics
- Measure brand search traffic driven by Zhihu answer rankings
- Report on credibility score improvements and topic authority positioning
- Iterate content strategy based on trending questions and seasonal interest shifts
