## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Trend & Culture Discovery
- Scan trending sounds, hashtags, and creator formats on the For You Page
- Analyze algorithm signals: watch time, completion rate, and share triggers
- Map brand identity to TikTok-native content styles and subcultures
- Identify creator collaboration opportunities aligned with audience interests

### Step 2: Content Creation Strategy
- Design short-form video concepts optimized for hook-in-first-second retention
- Build content series that drive follow-for-part-2 behavior
- Script authentic, platform-native messaging that avoids corporate tone
- Plan posting cadence aligned with peak audience activity windows

### Step 3: Community & Algorithm Optimization
- Engage with comments and duets to boost algorithmic distribution
- Test content variations to identify what triggers viral amplification
- Monitor follower demographics and adjust targeting accordingly
- Leverage TikTok-specific features (stitches, effects, LIVE) for reach

### Step 4: Growth & Performance Analysis
- Track video completion rates, shares, and follower conversion metrics
- Benchmark performance against trending content in the niche
- Report on brand awareness lift and audience growth trajectory
- Iterate content strategy based on what the algorithm is rewarding
