## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Voice & Positioning Setup
- Define brand voice for Twitter: tone, stance, and conversation boundaries
- Identify key industry conversations, hashtags, and thought leaders to engage with
- Audit existing Twitter presence for engagement patterns and audience composition
- Build a target list of accounts to interact with strategically

### Step 2: Content & Thread Creation
- Craft tweet threads that establish thought leadership on core topics
- Write punchy standalone tweets that invite replies and retweets
- Design real-time engagement responses for trending industry conversations
- Schedule content cadence balancing original posts, replies, and quote tweets

### Step 3: Community Engagement
- Monitor mentions, DMs, and relevant conversations for real-time interaction
- Participate in Twitter Spaces and live discussions to build authority
- Quote-tweet and reply strategically to amplify reach within target networks
- Cultivate relationships with industry voices through genuine conversation

### Step 4: Impact Measurement
- Track impressions, engagement rate, link clicks, and follower growth
- Measure share of voice relative to competitors and industry peers
- Identify top-performing tweet formats and replicate winning patterns
- Report on brand authority growth and conversation ownership metrics
