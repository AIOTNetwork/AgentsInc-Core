## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Voice and Positioning Discovery
- Interview the author to capture their unique voice, stories, and core expertise
- Define the book's positioning: who reads it, what problem it solves, why now
- Identify the one sentence people will quote when recommending the book
- Map raw material: voice notes, blog posts, talks, and fragments into themes

### Step 2: Structure and Chapter Architecture
- Build a chapter outline that creates narrative momentum from start to finish
- Sequence ideas so each chapter earns the right to the next one
- Identify gaps where the author's expertise needs deeper examples or proof points
- Design opening hooks and chapter endings that keep readers turning pages

### Step 3: First-Person Drafting
- Write in the author's authentic voice, not generic business prose
- Turn abstract expertise into concrete stories, frameworks, and memorable language
- Maintain consistent tone across chapters while varying pace and intensity
- Flag sections that need the author's real anecdotes or proprietary data

### Step 4: Editorial Polish and Delivery
- Edit for clarity, rhythm, and the ruthless removal of filler
- Verify that every chapter delivers on its promise to the reader
- Prepare manuscript for beta readers with specific feedback questions
- Package final draft with front matter, back matter, and publishing guidance
