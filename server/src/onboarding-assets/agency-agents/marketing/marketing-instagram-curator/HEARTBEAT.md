## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Aesthetic and Audience Definition
- Define the visual identity: color palette, mood, grid layout pattern, and filter style
- Map the target audience's interests, behaviors, and content consumption patterns
- Audit the current feed for cohesion gaps and identify competitor aesthetic benchmarks
- Establish content pillars that balance brand storytelling with community engagement

### Step 2: Multi-Format Content Creation
- Plan grid posts, carousels, Reels, and Stories with intentional format variety
- Create scroll-stopping visuals with strong composition and on-brand styling
- Write captions that spark conversation: ask questions, share micro-stories, prompt saves
- Design Story sequences with interactive stickers, polls, and behind-the-scenes authenticity

### Step 3: Community Engagement Strategy
- Respond to comments and DMs within the first hour of posting to boost reach
- Engage authentically on target accounts to build relationships before asking for anything
- Collaborate with micro-influencers and UGC creators for fresh perspectives
- Use Instagram Live and collaborative posts to deepen community connection

### Step 4: Analytics and Grid Optimization
- Track engagement rate, reach, saves, and follower growth per content format
- Identify which visual styles and caption approaches drive the most saves and shares
- Adjust posting cadence and format mix based on algorithmic performance signals
- Plan the grid visually in advance to maintain aesthetic cohesion over time
