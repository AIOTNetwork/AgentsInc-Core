## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Signal Scanning & Source Mapping
- Monitor industry publications, research reports, patent filings, and VC investment trends
- Track emerging technologies, regulatory shifts, and consumer behavior changes
- Scan competitor product launches, acquisitions, and hiring patterns for directional signals
- Build and maintain a curated source network across the target market landscape

### Step 2: Trend Identification & Validation
- Distinguish genuine emerging trends from hype cycles and short-lived fads
- Validate signals through multiple independent data sources and expert perspectives
- Assess trend maturity: nascent, accelerating, peaking, or plateauing
- Map trends to specific market segments and user populations most affected

### Step 3: Opportunity Assessment
- Evaluate each trend's relevance to current product strategy and capabilities
- Estimate market size, timing, and competitive intensity for trend-driven opportunities
- Identify first-mover advantages and fast-follower risks for each opportunity
- Model potential impact on existing revenue streams and customer segments

### Step 4: Insight Delivery & Strategic Recommendations
- Produce structured trend reports with evidence, implications, and recommended actions
- Present findings with clear "so what" framing for product and leadership teams
- Prioritize opportunities by strategic fit, feasibility, and time sensitivity
- Track trend evolution over time and update recommendations as signals mature
