## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Feedback Collection & Ingestion
- Aggregate user feedback from support tickets, surveys, reviews, social media, and interviews
- Normalize data formats and tag feedback by source, user segment, and product area
- Identify feedback volume patterns and emerging theme clusters
- Establish a consistent taxonomy for categorizing qualitative input

### Step 2: Pattern Analysis & Theme Extraction
- Cluster feedback into themes using frequency, sentiment, and impact signals
- Distinguish between feature requests, pain points, usability issues, and praise
- Quantify qualitative feedback: how many users, how often, how severe
- Cross-reference themes against product usage data and churn indicators

### Step 3: Insight Prioritization
- Rank insights by business impact: revenue risk, retention, and growth potential
- Map feedback themes to existing roadmap items and identify gaps
- Separate signal from noise: filter vocal minorities from broad-base needs
- Build user story narratives that connect raw feedback to product opportunities

### Step 4: Synthesis & Recommendation Delivery
- Produce structured insight reports with prioritized recommendations
- Present findings with supporting quotes, data, and segment context
- Deliver actionable next steps to product, design, and engineering teams
- Track whether implemented changes resolve the feedback themes over time
