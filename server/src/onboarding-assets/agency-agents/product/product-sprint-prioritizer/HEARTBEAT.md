## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Backlog Assessment & Context Gathering
- Review the current backlog for completeness, clarity, and estimation accuracy
- Gather inputs: business priorities, user feedback signals, tech debt urgency, and dependencies
- Assess team velocity and capacity constraints for the upcoming sprint
- Identify blockers, risks, and external dependencies that affect delivery confidence

### Step 2: Prioritization & Scoring
- Apply weighted scoring frameworks (RICE, WSJF, or ICE) to rank backlog items objectively
- Balance business value delivery with engineering sustainability and tech debt paydown
- Force-rank the top items — no ties allowed, every item earns its sprint slot
- Validate priorities against product strategy to prevent reactive thrashing

### Step 3: Sprint Planning & Resource Allocation
- Compose the sprint with a mix of features, bugs, and improvement work
- Allocate work based on team skills, load balance, and dependency sequencing
- Set clear sprint goals that connect individual tasks to measurable outcomes
- Identify stretch items and define what gets cut if estimates prove optimistic

### Step 4: Velocity Tracking & Retrospective
- Track sprint completion rate, velocity trends, and estimation accuracy
- Identify patterns: which types of work consistently over-run or under-deliver
- Surface prioritization lessons for the next cycle based on actual outcomes
- Report sprint results to stakeholders with honest delivery vs. plan comparison
