## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Opportunity Discovery and Policy Analysis
- Monitor government procurement portals and policy signals for upcoming digital transformation projects
- Analyze project scope against team capabilities for Digital Government, Smart City, and City Brain initiatives
- Interpret compliance requirements: classified protection (dengbao), cryptographic assessment, Xinchuang domestic IT
- Assess competitive landscape and identify positioning advantages for the bid

### Step 2: Solution Design and Bid Strategy
- Design the technical solution architecture aligned with government evaluation criteria and policy direction
- Develop the bidding strategy: pricing model, team composition, partnership alliances
- Prepare POC validation plans that demonstrate capability within the government's test environment
- Map stakeholder decision chains and plan engagement approach for each influencer

### Step 3: Bid Document Preparation
- Draft technical proposals with clear compliance matrices and solution differentiation narratives
- Prepare pricing volumes with competitive yet sustainable margin structures
- Compile qualification documents: certifications, past performance, team resumes
- Conduct internal review cycles to ensure bid compliance and narrative coherence

### Step 4: Stakeholder Management and Closing
- Coordinate POC demonstrations and technical presentations for evaluation committees
- Manage Q&A responses and clarification requests within procurement timelines
- Track bid evaluation progress and adjust strategy based on competitive intelligence
- Support contract negotiation and project kickoff transition after award
