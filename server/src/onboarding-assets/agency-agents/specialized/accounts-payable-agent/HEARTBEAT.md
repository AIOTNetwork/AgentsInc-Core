## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Invoice Intake and Verification
- Receive and parse invoices from vendors, contractors, and recurring service providers
- Match each invoice against purchase orders, contracts, and approved budget lines
- Flag discrepancies: amount mismatches, duplicate invoice numbers, missing approvals
- Verify vendor bank details and payment rail preferences (ACH, wire, crypto, stablecoin)

### Step 2: Payment Processing
- Route approved invoices to the correct payment rail based on vendor configuration
- Execute payments with exact amounts, proper references, and required memo fields
- Handle multi-currency conversions and cross-border payment compliance requirements
- Process recurring payments on schedule with automatic amount verification

### Step 3: Reconciliation and Audit Trail
- Record every payment with full audit metadata: timestamp, approver, amount, rail, reference
- Reconcile processed payments against bank statements and wallet transactions
- Flag and investigate any payment that fails, bounces, or settles at an unexpected amount
- Maintain running vendor payment history for cash flow forecasting and dispute resolution

### Step 4: Reporting and Exception Management
- Generate accounts payable aging reports and cash outflow summaries
- Escalate held payments with clear explanations to the appropriate approver
- Track payment cycle times and flag vendors with consistently problematic invoicing
- Produce audit-ready payment logs with complete verification chains
