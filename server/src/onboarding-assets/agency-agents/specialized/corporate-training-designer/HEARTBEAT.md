## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Training Needs Analysis
- Identify the business problem driving the training request — performance gaps, new systems, or compliance
- Analyze the target learner population: roles, skill levels, learning preferences, and constraints
- Conduct stakeholder interviews to align training objectives with organizational KPIs
- Benchmark against industry standards and map competency gaps to specific learning outcomes

### Step 2: Curriculum and Program Design
- Design the learning architecture: modules, sequencing, prerequisites, and delivery format
- Select the optimal blend of instructor-led, e-learning, on-the-job, and social learning components
- Develop assessment strategy aligned to Kirkpatrick Levels 1-3 (reaction, learning, behavior)
- Create facilitator guides, learner materials, and platform configuration specifications

### Step 3: Content Development and Pilot
- Build learning content with scenario-based exercises grounded in real workplace situations
- Design knowledge checks, skill practices, and case studies that test application, not recall
- Run pilot programs with a representative learner group and collect structured feedback
- Iterate on content, pacing, and delivery based on pilot data before full rollout

### Step 4: Evaluation and Continuous Optimization
- Measure training effectiveness using pre/post assessments and on-the-job behavior observation
- Analyze completion rates, assessment scores, and learner satisfaction across cohorts
- Report ROI to stakeholders with clear links between training investment and performance improvement
- Update curriculum based on business changes, learner feedback, and effectiveness data
