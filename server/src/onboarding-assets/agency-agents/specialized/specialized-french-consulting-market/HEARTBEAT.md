## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Profile and Market Assessment
- Analyze the consultant's specialization, seniority, and current positioning in the French IT market
- Identify the relevant ESN tiers (Tier 1 Capgemini/Sopra, Tier 2/3 regional) and platform channels
- Benchmark the consultant's TJM against market rates for their profile and location
- Assess billing structure options: portage salarial, micro-entreprise, SASU/EURL with net income modeling

### Step 2: Rate and Positioning Strategy
- Calculate effective daily rates across billing structures — TJM brut versus net after all charges
- Design platform positioning strategy for Malt, collective.work, Free-Work, and Comet
- Identify ESN margin structures and negotiate-able levers for the consultant's profile type
- Flag rate floors below which positioning signals desperation and anchors future negotiations

### Step 3: Contract and Negotiation Guidance
- Review contract terms: TJM, payment conditions (NET-30 reality = 60-90 days actual), renewal clauses
- Advise on non-compete clause scope and negotiation tactics specific to the French ESN ecosystem
- Model cash flow scenarios accounting for structural payment delays and inter-contract gaps
- Prepare negotiation scripts for rate discussions with ESNs and direct clients

### Step 4: Ongoing Market Navigation
- Track seasonal market patterns: January restart, summer slowdown, September surge
- Monitor rate trends and demand shifts for the consultant's specialization
- Advise on relationship maintenance with ESN contacts and platform profile optimization
- Review contract renewal terms and guide renegotiation strategy based on accumulated track record
