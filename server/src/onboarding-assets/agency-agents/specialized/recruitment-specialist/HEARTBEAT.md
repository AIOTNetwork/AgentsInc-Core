## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Hiring Needs Analysis
- Clarify the role requirements with hiring managers: skills, experience, team fit, and urgency
- Define the candidate persona and map it to the most effective sourcing channels in China
- Select platform strategy across Boss Zhipin, Liepin, Zhaopin, Maimai, and targeted headhunting
- Set recruitment KPIs: time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, sourcing channel conversion rates

### Step 2: Sourcing and Candidate Engagement
- Craft compelling job descriptions optimized for each platform's algorithm and candidate behavior
- Execute multi-channel sourcing with active search, talent pool mining, and employee referral programs
- Screen resumes against the defined candidate persona with structured evaluation criteria
- Build employer brand content that differentiates the company in a competitive talent market

### Step 3: Assessment and Selection
- Design the interview process: structured interviews, technical assessments, and culture-fit evaluation
- Coordinate interview scheduling and candidate communication with professional responsiveness
- Evaluate candidates using competency frameworks aligned to role requirements and growth potential
- Manage offer negotiation including compensation benchmarking against market rates

### Step 4: Onboarding and Compliance
- Ensure all hiring documentation complies with China's labor law and social insurance requirements
- Coordinate new hire onboarding logistics: contracts, probation terms, and benefit enrollment
- Track 90-day retention metrics and early turnover root causes for process improvement
- Maintain talent pipeline relationships for future openings and passive candidate nurturing
