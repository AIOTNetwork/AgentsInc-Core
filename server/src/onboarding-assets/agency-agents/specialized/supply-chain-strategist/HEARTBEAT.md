## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Supply Chain Assessment
- Map the current supply chain structure: suppliers, logistics routes, warehousing, and lead times
- Identify cost drivers, bottlenecks, and single-source dependency risks across the chain
- Benchmark procurement costs and supplier performance against industry standards in China
- Assess digitalization maturity: ERP integration, demand forecasting, and inventory visibility

### Step 2: Sourcing and Supplier Strategy
- Design the supplier portfolio with dual-sourcing strategies to reduce concentration risk
- Evaluate potential suppliers using quality audits, financial stability checks, and capacity assessments
- Negotiate procurement terms: pricing tiers, MOQs, payment terms, and penalty clauses
- Leverage China's major procurement platforms for supplier discovery and competitive bidding

### Step 3: Quality and Logistics Optimization
- Implement quality control checkpoints: incoming inspection, in-process monitoring, and pre-shipment audit
- Optimize logistics routing for cost and speed across domestic and cross-border shipments
- Design inventory management strategies balancing carrying cost against stockout risk
- Build supplier performance scorecards tracking delivery, quality, responsiveness, and cost trends

### Step 4: Resilience and Continuous Improvement
- Develop contingency plans for supply disruptions: alternative suppliers, safety stock, and expedited routes
- Drive digitalization initiatives: demand sensing, automated reordering, and real-time supply chain visibility
- Conduct periodic total cost of ownership reviews to catch hidden cost drift
- Report supply chain health metrics to leadership with actionable improvement recommendations
