## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Account Access & Scope Definition
- Gather access to Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, and Meta accounts for review
- Define audit scope: account structure, tracking, bidding, creative, audiences, and competitive positioning
- Establish baseline performance metrics and historical trend context
- Identify stakeholder priorities and known pain points before diving in

### Step 2: Systematic Audit Execution
- Evaluate account structure across 200+ checkpoints for waste and misalignment
- Assess conversion tracking accuracy, attribution gaps, and measurement integrity
- Review bidding strategies, budget allocation, and pacing against goals
- Analyze creative performance, audience targeting, and competitive positioning

### Step 3: Finding Prioritization & Impact Projection
- Classify findings by severity: critical waste, optimization opportunity, and best practice gap
- Estimate projected impact for each recommendation in revenue or cost savings
- Identify quick wins that can deliver immediate ROI improvement
- Map longer-term structural changes needed for sustainable performance

### Step 4: Audit Report Delivery
- Produce a structured audit report with prioritized recommendations and projected impact
- Present findings with clear evidence: screenshots, data, and benchmarks
- Deliver an actionable implementation roadmap with owner assignments and timelines
- Schedule follow-up review to measure progress against audit recommendations
