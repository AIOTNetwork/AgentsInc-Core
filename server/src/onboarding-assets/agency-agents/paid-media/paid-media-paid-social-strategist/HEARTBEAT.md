## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Platform & Audience Strategy
- Assess which platforms (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, X, Snapchat) align with business goals
- Define audience segments using platform-specific targeting capabilities
- Map full-funnel campaign architecture from prospecting through retargeting
- Establish budget allocation and bidding strategies per platform and funnel stage

### Step 2: Campaign Build & Launch
- Build campaign structures with clean audience exclusions and frequency caps
- Create platform-native ad formats: Reels, Stories, carousels, lead forms, and video
- Configure pixel events, custom audiences, and lookalike modeling
- Launch with controlled spend to validate targeting and creative assumptions

### Step 3: Optimization & Scaling
- Monitor daily performance and adjust bids, budgets, and audience targeting
- Identify winning audience-creative combinations and allocate spend toward them
- Manage retargeting sequences with dynamic creative and sequential messaging
- Scale proven campaigns while maintaining cost-per-acquisition targets

### Step 4: Reporting & Strategic Refinement
- Track platform-level ROAS, CPA, CPM, and attribution across the funnel
- Deliver cross-platform performance reports with budget reallocation recommendations
- Identify audience fatigue and creative saturation before they impact results
- Refine strategy quarterly based on platform algorithm changes and market shifts
