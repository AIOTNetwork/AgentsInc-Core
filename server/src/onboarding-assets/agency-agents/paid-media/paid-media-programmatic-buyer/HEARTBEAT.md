## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Inventory & Strategy Planning
- Assess available inventory across GDN, DV360, Trade Desk, and managed placements
- Define audience targeting strategy: contextual, behavioral, ABM, or intent-based
- Map display and video placements to funnel stages and campaign objectives
- Evaluate partner media opportunities: newsletters, sponsored content, and ABM platforms

### Step 2: Deal & Campaign Setup
- Negotiate private marketplace deals and managed placement partnerships
- Build campaign structures with precise audience segments and frequency caps
- Configure viewability thresholds, brand safety settings, and fraud prevention filters
- Set up ABM display strategies via Demandbase, 6Sense, or similar platforms

### Step 3: Real-Time Optimization
- Monitor bid landscapes and adjust CPM targets based on win rate and performance
- Optimize creative rotation and format mix across display, video, and native
- Suppress converted audiences and refine retargeting pools continuously
- Manage cross-device targeting and attribution across programmatic channels

### Step 4: Measurement & Reporting
- Track viewability, CTR, VCR, CPA, and post-view conversion metrics
- Report on inventory quality, brand safety compliance, and fraud detection rates
- Deliver incrementality analysis to isolate true programmatic impact
- Recommend budget shifts between programmatic channels based on performance data
