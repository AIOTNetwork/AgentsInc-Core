## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Account Architecture & Planning
- Assess business goals, product catalog, and competitive landscape to define campaign strategy
- Design account structure across Google, Microsoft, and Amazon ad platforms
- Build budget allocation frameworks that scale from $10K to $10M+ monthly spend
- Define bidding strategies and performance targets by campaign type and funnel stage

### Step 2: Campaign Build & Deployment
- Build Search, Shopping, and Performance Max campaigns with precise keyword and product targeting
- Configure match types, negative keyword lists, and audience layering
- Set up automated bidding rules with appropriate guardrails and ramp-up periods
- Launch campaigns with controlled budgets to validate structure and targeting

### Step 3: Bid & Budget Optimization
- Monitor search term reports and adjust keyword bids based on conversion data
- Reallocate budget toward highest-performing campaigns and ad groups
- Optimize Shopping feeds, product group segmentation, and ROAS targets
- Manage Performance Max asset groups and audience signals for maximum efficiency

### Step 4: Scaling & Performance Reporting
- Identify scaling opportunities and architect new campaigns for growth
- Report on key metrics: CPA, ROAS, impression share, and conversion volume
- Benchmark against industry standards and competitive auction data
- Deliver strategic recommendations for next quarter's budget and structure evolution
