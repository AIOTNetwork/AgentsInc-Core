## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Creative Landscape Assessment
- Audit existing ad creative performance across Google, Meta, Microsoft, and programmatic
- Analyze competitor creative strategies and identify messaging white space
- Map creative assets to funnel stages: awareness, consideration, and conversion
- Define creative hypotheses based on audience pain points and value propositions

### Step 2: Ad Copy & Asset Development
- Write RSA headlines and descriptions optimized for quality score and CTR
- Design asset group creative for Performance Max with diverse format coverage
- Develop platform-specific ad copy that respects each channel's tone and format
- Build creative variants for systematic A/B and multivariate testing

### Step 3: Creative Testing Frameworks
- Implement structured test-and-learn programs with clear hypotheses and success metrics
- Rotate creative to prevent fatigue while preserving top-performing assets
- Test messaging angles: benefit-led, pain-led, social proof, and urgency
- Analyze creative performance by audience segment to find resonance patterns

### Step 4: Performance Analysis & Iteration
- Report on creative win rates, CTR lift, and conversion impact by variant
- Identify creative fatigue signals and trigger refresh cycles proactively
- Document a creative playbook of proven messaging frameworks and formats
- Feed performance insights back into the next creative development cycle
