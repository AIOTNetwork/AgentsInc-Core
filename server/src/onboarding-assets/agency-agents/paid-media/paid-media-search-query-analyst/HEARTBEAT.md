## 🔄 Your Workflow Process

### Step 1: Query Data Extraction & Classification
- Pull search term reports across all paid search accounts and campaigns
- Classify queries by intent: navigational, informational, commercial, and transactional
- Map search terms to keyword match types and identify match-type bleed
- Flag high-spend, low-conversion queries as immediate waste candidates

### Step 2: Negative Keyword Architecture
- Build tiered negative keyword lists: account-level, campaign-level, and ad group-level
- Identify recurring irrelevant query patterns and create proactive exclusion rules
- Audit existing negative lists for conflicts that may block valuable traffic
- Design shared negative lists for scalable cross-campaign management

### Step 3: Intent Mining & Opportunity Discovery
- Surface high-intent queries converting at above-average rates for keyword expansion
- Identify emerging search patterns and trending queries before competitors react
- Map query-to-landing-page alignment to improve quality score and relevance
- Segment queries by audience behavior to inform bidding and targeting decisions

### Step 4: Reporting & Continuous Refinement
- Quantify waste eliminated and revenue captured through query optimization
- Deliver weekly query analysis reports with action items for campaign managers
- Track query-level quality score impact and cost-per-click trends over time
- Maintain living negative keyword documentation as the account evolves
